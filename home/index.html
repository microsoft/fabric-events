    One business signal.
    Many ways to act.

    A sales dip. A newly placed order. Recognize what matters and share the facts teams need to act or analyze.

    Your workloads already detect important business situations. Business Events represent those signals through a shared, schema-defined contract, so teams and applications can reuse the same business meaning and respond independently.
